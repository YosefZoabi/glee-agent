The opening ask is underpriced

Next experiment

never_concede_below · opening_demand · needs a real A/B

With the accept side closed this is where bargaining is still losing. In open games we ask 0.500 and it is signed 55–100% of the time; in the δ 0.80/0.80 bounded cell we ask 0.597 and it is signed 95.2%. An offer taken 95% of the time is priced too low. This is SPE played against a field that is not playing SPE — the clamp rescues us from the theory's 0.0 and then stops.

Evidence

Why it needs a window
Whether they accept a higher ask is their move, so no replay can price it. This is the one open question arithmetic cannot answer — it needs an arm and a control.

Shipped on

The open-game walk-down exists to break a deadlock, and at δ=1.0 there is no deadlock to break: the offer is worth exactly as much on round 97 as on round 1, and the round-97 collapse is already underneath it. settle_early — charge a rejection what it costs

Measured, then unbundled

settle_early_on · rounds_to_settle 3→9 · realistic_counter_share 0.45→0.25 · min_accept_share 0.35→0.50

The biggest bargaining finding. The share we negotiate is right — at δ=0.95 we sign a nominal 0.667 against an SPE of 0.673 — but we take six rounds to get there and our own inflation eats 23% of it, landing at 0.49 where the field banks 0.64. Our clock, not their stubbornness.

Evidence
23,617 refused offers, each an exact paired counterfactual (accepting is unilateral and terminal, so "sign now" is share × δ^(round-1)). No survivorship — no-deals bank zero and are still counted.
Break-even
δ=0.90 open 0.20 vs bar 0.505 · δ=0.90/12 0.20 vs 0.505 · δ=0.95 open 0.20 vs 0.624 · δ=1.00 open hidden 0.30 vs 0.500. Cost −0.0055 of pot per game.
Simulated
+0.031 percentile (~+245 rating) against a ±45 noise floor from six worlds. Tuned bar lands closer to the real break-even in 3 of 3 cells that move, further in none.
Live test
run41 + run42 — glee-agent, 7aidara, Alpha. 1,285 bargaining games, read by replaying the shipped code over every one of them: 4,272 of 4,272 logged decisions reproduced exactly.
Verdict
The bundle gained +0.0458 of pot on 108 games (7.7σ) — and unbundling shows all of it came from min_accept_share. rounds_to_settle 3→9 and counter_share 0.45→0.25 each produced zero measurable gain and nothing but unmeasurable early signings. The two "settle sooner" knobs were dropped; the floor shipped alone.
Retracted
The premise. Median settlement is round 2, not six — the whole-corpus figure did not describe the current build. The simulator's rounds_to_settle sweep (12→+76) pointed the wrong way and did not survive contact with real games.

Negotiation

strongest family · ~2400–2600

stonewall_needs_endgame — don't cave while refusing is free

Shipped on

stonewall_needs_endgame = True · stonewall_offers = 6

My idea — the opponent is reading us as a reliable signer and repeating the same price to exploit it; keep refusing. On the 6th identical price we used to sign. Now we wait until refusing actually costs something — the end of a bounded game, or the round cap of an open one.

Live test
run37 on 7aidara, 5h, against four controls
Result
12 better, 11 exactly equal, 0 worse. Sign test p = 0.00049 (3.5σ). Caving would have paid 0.0604 of valuation; holding banked 0.1162. All 23 closed — no no-deals, no walk-aways.
Method
Paired within-game: at the gate the price is known, so caving's payoff is arithmetic. The four controls validated it — they cave on sight, and across 173 games the largest deviation from the predicted value was 2.3e-9.
Cost
Negotiation throughput ~20% (96 games/slot vs 120–130). Walk-away risk only bounded at ~12% by 0-of-23.

rung_aware — price against the valuation pool

Shipped on

rung_aware = True · rung_shade 0.15 · rung_last_word_shade 0.01

Valuations are drawn from a known pool — {80, 100, 120, 150} × {1, 100, 10000}. The ladder walks the tradable rungs from the top down, because a rung refused costs only the round it took to ask.

Structural
A zone of agreement always exists (18 distinct pairs, all same-scale, seller < buyer), so _no_zone_of_agreement is dead code. Negotiation carries no inflation — payoff is |price − value|, confirmed against the cache.

hide_rung_from_last_word

Premise retracted

hide_rung_from_last_word = False · kept, off

From my rung-signalling question. Shipped on the claim that an ask made when the last price is theirs "is never signed anyway, so raising it is free" — 0 signings in 715 games.

Defect

Buyer-seat counterfactuals

Structurally impossible

standing constraint

Quality is revealed only on rounds we bought — 28,309 revealed against 34,129 not. Any buyer-side "what if we had passed" is unanswerable, because the outcome we would compare against was never observed.

Consequence
Persuasion buyer-seat changes cannot be validated offline. Seller seat only.

Method & tooling

how anything gets decided

Offline simulator

Built, in use

scratchpad: corpus.py · oppfit.py · engine.py · opponents.py · sweep*.py

My idea. No API. Configurations drawn from the real mix of all 15,788 bargaining games; our seat played by the shipped code; the other seat by a field fitted to real opponents plus eight adversarial archetypes.

Calibration
Reproduces median settlement round per cell; scores +0.03 of pot optimistic on level. Good for relative comparison, useless for absolute claims.
Noise floor
±45 rating per world at n=4,000, measured by re-running the shipped policy on six worlds. Any claimed gain must clear it.
Rule
Never ship on simulator evidence alone. It chooses which arm deserves a real window.

Replay the flag before running it

Mandatory pre-flight

scratchpad: replay43.py · unbundle.py · sweep43.py

The turn logs are enough to re-run the shipped decision code exactly. Rebuild state["history"] from the turn sequence — the log keeps only its length — then call the real _make_decision twice with the flag flipped. It reproduced 4,272 of 4,272 bargaining decisions and 13,056 of 13,056 negotiation ones. Zero mismatches: this is the agent, not a model of it.

Why it exists
run42 spent three hours and 1,956 games on three arms. Two of them changed zero decisions. A replay would have said so before launch, for free.
The rule
Count how many decisions a candidate changes — not how often its condition fires. Both dud arms were sized on the second question.
What it settles outright
A flag that only makes us refuse more needs no window at all: accepting is unilateral and terminal, so the alternative is arithmetic. That is how the whole accept-side sweep was decided without a single game.
What it cannot settle
A flag that makes us accept earlier. The opponent then plays on and the counterfactual is unrecoverable — that half needs a real arm and a control.

Paired within-game counterfactual

Standing technique

the only measurement that has worked

At a decision point the price on the table is known, so the payoff of the other choice is arithmetic, not inference. Accepting is unilateral and terminal, so every refused offer is its own control.

Why it matters
It made the stonewall result readable at n=23 and the bargaining diagnosis solid at n=23,617 — where Elo said nothing at either sample size.

Elo as a measurement

Do not use

game_rating = 2000 + 8000×(percentile − 0.5)

One percentile point is 80 rating points. Two identical builds diverged 213 points in bargaining and 118 in negotiation over the same 5-hour window.

Rule
Read mechanism first, never Elo. Run three controls, never one.

Denominators conditioned on the answer

Three occurrences

the recurring failure mode

Three measurements have been wrong the same way — the filter that built the sample already excluded everything that would have contradicted it.

1
"They concede 67% however long we hold" — computed over closed deals; the games where they walked were invisible.
2
"Deal rate 1.000 with a ZOPA" — a ZOPA is only computable from a closed deal. True by construction.
3
"Penultimate ask signed 0 of 715" — required a final-round entry, which no game has if the ask closed it earlier.
Test
Ask what a game must do to enter the denominator, then ask whether the event being measured would have prevented that.
